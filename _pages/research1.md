Research
===
Research Journey
---
My academic and research journey has been driven by a passion for solving complex, real-world problems in chemical engineering using Artificial Intelligence (AI) methodologies. I have a strong conviction that AI will transform our world for the better, and that engineers will be at the forefront of shaping this future. This belief drives my desire to play a role in this exciting new frontier in the chemical engineering domain. My doctoral work has provided me with a robust foundation of <b>utilizing AI in material property and process modelling, simulation, control and optimization</b>, and I am now eager to apply it to address the next generation of challenges in this field. 

PhD Research
---
During my Ph.D. at the National Chemical Laboratory (NCL), Pune, India, my research focused on the design and development of Predictive AI-based methodologies for chemical engineering applications. In a collaborative team-work for a period of eight years at the Artificial Intelligent Systems Group (AISG) at NCL, Pune, I developed comprehensive knowledge of AI concepts while gaining practical experience across diverse platforms. My early work utilized user-friendly <b>Predictive AI-based modelling</b> packages such as <b>(IBM SPSS, RapidMiner, Eureqa Formulize, MATLAB)</b>, which provided essential context. Alongside I developed codes for the Artificial Immune-based Optimization methodology, namely Clonal Selection Algorithm (CLONALG) for optimization of adsorptive removal of Chromium using synthetic polymer resins and genetic programming (GP)-based soft-sensor models used in Model Predictive Control (MPC) simulation of a non-linear continuous polystyrene producing reactor in MATLAB. 
Post-PhD Research
---

Post-PhD I have worked on the multi-objective optimization of High Shear Wet Granulation process for better pharmaceutical product flow properties. Recently my interests also shifted to <b>Accelerated Materials Discovery (AMD)</b > and <b>Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)</b>. AMD solely depends on utilizing the AI/ML methods to accelerate tailored materials discovery having desired properties. In this I'm focusing on the <b>integration of AI (Predictive and Generative) methods</b> to significantly enhance performance, speed up material innovation, and achieve more accurate, efficient molecular dynamics simulations. So, currently I am working on the development of AI-based models for predicting the boiling point of organic compounds by MD simulations in LAMMPS. 

In summary I'm deeply passionate about leveraging cutting-edge AI techniques to advance Chemical Engineering Research. My current focus is more on exploring how <b>Generative AI</b> can be practically applied and integrated into the Chemical Engineering Research Workflows for AMD using the full power and flexibility of <b>Python's AI ecosystem</b> for developing custom, state-of-the-art models.
